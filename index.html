About Sampanna Edu

Built for students who want a clearer way to prepare.

Sampanna Edu is designed around simple navigation and exam-relevant learning: choose a subject, open a chapter, study notes, practice MCQs and take mock tests.

Students simply enter their real name and class to use the platform. No student Google login is required. Your profile and practice history stay in this browser, and the platform remains lightweight and accessible on phones, tablets and computers.
